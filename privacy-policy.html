Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

In short
  • We use your receipts only to run ReturnGuard for you. We don't sell your data and don't show ads.
  • Searching your photos for receipts happens on your phone; only photos you pick are uploaded.
  • Gmail access is read-only, limited to purchase emails you choose, and never used for advertising.
  • You can export or delete your data at any time.

1. Who we are

ReturnGuard ("we", "us") is responsible for the personal data described in this policy. Contact: o.snijders.os@gmail.com.

2. Data we process

DataWhy
Account: email address, name, sign-in provider (Google, Apple, Facebook or email)To create your account and sync your receipts between devices.
Receipts: photos, PDFs and the details read from them (store, date, items, prices, receipt number)To store your receipts, calculate return deadlines and warranties, and produce reports.
Return and complaint requests you start, and their statusTo help you follow up and remind you.
Purchase emails you choose to import or shareTo create receipts from order confirmations and invoices.
Subscription status (via an anonymous app user ID)To unlock Premium features.

We do not use advertising trackers or sell personal data. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

3. Your photos

When you use Find receipts in my photos, ReturnGuard scans recent photos on your device using on-device text recognition to spot likely receipts. Nothing is uploaded during this search. Only the photos you select are uploaded and read like a scanned receipt. We only request access to images, not videos or location data.

4. Gmail

If you choose Import from Gmail, we ask for read-only access (gmail.readonly). ReturnGuard then searches for purchase-related emails from the last 90 days and shows you the subjects. Only the emails you select are read, and their text is sent to our receipt-reading service to extract the purchase details. We keep the resulting receipt data, not the emails themselves. We never send, delete or change emails.

ReturnGuard's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to train general AI models. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. Legal bases (GDPR)

We process your data to perform our contract with you (providing the app), with your consent (Gmail and photo access, which you can withdraw at any time), and for our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

7. How long we keep data

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete a receipt it is removed from our systems; when you delete your account, all your data is deleted within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it.

8. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, object to processing, and withdraw consent. Most of this can be done in the app; otherwise email us. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority; in the Netherlands that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

9. Children

ReturnGuard is not intended for children under 16.

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and stored with providers that encrypt data at rest. Access to your receipts is limited to your account.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
