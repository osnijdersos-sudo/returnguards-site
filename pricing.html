Pricing questions

How does the free trial work?

Where your app store offers it, Premium is free for 7 days. You won't be charged if you cancel before the trial ends. Cancel in Google Play or App Store subscriptions.

What happens when I reach 10 receipts on Free?

Your existing receipts, reminders and the return assistant keep working. You can add more receipts next month, or upgrade to Premium for unlimited scanning.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly?

Yes, in your app store's subscription settings. The change takes effect at your next renewal.

How do refunds work?

Payments are handled by Google Play and the App Store, so refunds follow their policies. You can request one from the store, or contact us and we'll help.
