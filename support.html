Support

How can we help?

Email us at o.snijders.os@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days.

Scanning

The receipt wasn't read correctly

Lay the receipt flat in good light with the whole receipt in the frame. You can fix any detail by tapping the pencil icon on the receipt. Long receipts can be photographed in two parts.

Can I add order emails and PDF invoices?

Yes. In Gmail, Outlook or your files app, tap Share and choose ReturnGuard. Premium users can also import purchase emails from Gmail directly via Add → Import from Gmail.

Return policies

The return window looks wrong

Open the receipt and tap Re-check to look up the latest policy, or Correct to enter it yourself. Your correction is remembered for future receipts from that store.

Where does the policy come from?

In this order: the receipt itself, your own correction, the store's official website for your country, our store database, and finally a clearly marked estimate. The source is shown on every receipt.

Reminders

I'm not getting reminders

Make sure notifications are allowed for ReturnGuard in your phone's settings. On some Android phones you also need to turn off battery optimisation for the app, otherwise scheduled reminders can be delayed.

Account & data

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app go to Account → Delete account. This removes your receipts, images and account from our systems within 30 days. You can also email us to request deletion.

How do I disconnect Gmail?

Remove ReturnGuard at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Receipts you already imported stay in the app until you delete them.

How do I cancel Premium?

Cancel in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions) or on iPhone in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. You keep Premium until the end of the paid period.
