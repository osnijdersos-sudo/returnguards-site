FAQ

Questions, answered

How accurate are the return policies?

ReturnGuard first uses any policy printed on your receipt, then checks the store's own website for the country you bought in, and shows the source link. If it can't verify a policy, it says so clearly instead of guessing. You can always correct a policy, and we'll remember it for that store.

Which countries and stores are supported?

Any store with a receipt. Policies are looked up per country, and the built-in legal rights cover the EU, the UK and Australia. Receipts in English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish and many other languages are read automatically.

What happens to my photos and emails?

Receipt photos you scan are sent securely to be read and are stored in your account. When you use "Find receipts in my photos", the search happens on your phone, and only the photos you choose are uploaded. Gmail import is read-only, only looks for purchase emails, and only reads the ones you select. See our privacy policy.

Is ReturnGuard legal advice?

No. ReturnGuard gives practical guidance based on store policies and consumer law, but it isn't a lawyer. Always check important deadlines with the store.

How do I cancel Premium?

Cancel anytime in Google Play or App Store subscriptions. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for.
