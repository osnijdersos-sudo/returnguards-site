FAQ

Questions, answered

Which stores does it work with?

Any store that gives you a receipt or an order confirmation: Target, Walmart, Best Buy, Amazon, Apple, Costco, The Home Depot, Nike and every small shop down the street. Return policies are looked up for the store and country you bought in.

How accurate are the return policies?

ReturnGuard first uses any policy printed on your receipt, then checks the store's own website and shows you the source link. If it can't verify a policy, it says so clearly instead of guessing. You can correct any policy, and it will remember that for the store.

Does it work with Amazon and other online orders?

Yes. Share an order confirmation email or PDF to ReturnGuard from Gmail, Outlook or your browser, or let ReturnGuard find them in your photos. Online orders get the same deadlines and reminders as in-store receipts.

What happens to my photos and emails?

When ReturnGuard looks for receipts in your photos, the search happens on your phone, and only the receipts you choose are uploaded. Gmail import is read-only, only looks for purchase emails, and only reads the ones you select. We never sell your data. See our privacy policy.

Is ReturnGuard legal advice?

No. ReturnGuard gives practical guidance based on store policies and consumer law, but it isn't a lawyer. For important purchases, double-check the deadline with the store.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature is free while we launch, with no ads and no subscription. If we ever add paid extras, everything you use today keeps working.
